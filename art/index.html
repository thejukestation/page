ART

this page covers two sides of my visual world: VISUAL ART — paintings, photography and the works of others that move me — and DIGITAL ART, where i create my own edits and artworks. certain images, colors or compositions can evoke emotions that are hard to explain — inspiring, comforting, or sometimes unsettling. i'm fascinated by how art can do that without a single word. platforms like PINTEREST helped expand that fascination and shape my visual taste.

DIGITAL ART

i've been into DIGITAL ART for about 5 years — and a lot of it starts with something analog. a photo, a scan, a texture. i take that and push it into something new. i've always worked in PAINT.NET — layering filters, making collages, cutting things out and building something new from scratch.

now, with the album and my new online presence, i want to push in a specific direction. i feel most at home in a style defined by hard edges, strong DITHERING and HALFTONES.
